
# MV/LV Electrical Network Design — Residential Subdivision (ASSAFA4)

End-of-studies internship project (Associate's Degree in Electrical Engineering) carried out at **ONEE** (Office National de l'Électricité et de l'Eau Potable / National Office of Electricity and Drinking Water), Electricity Branch, Agadir — Regional Distribution Directorate, Technical Division, Reasearch & Projects Department.

## Overview

This project presents a comprehensive electrification study for a new residential subdivision ("Lotissement ASSAFA4") in Inezgane Aït Melloul, Morocco. It covers the design and sizing of both the Medium Voltage (MV, 22 kV) and Low Voltage (LV, 400 V) distribution networks using underground cabling.

The objective was to determine and size the electrical infrastructure and equipment required to supply the subdivision — from the initial power demand assessment to the detailed cost estimation of the works, based on ONEE technical standards and approved price references.

## What the study covers

- **Power balance:** Estimation of the power demand for residential, commercial, educational, and public facilities based on building type and surface area.
- **Transformer selection:** Selection of a 400 kVA transformer based on the estimated total demand.
- **LV network design:** Load distribution among feeders, cable sizing based on nominal current, and voltage drop verification.
- **MV network design:** Extension of an existing 22 kV line to supply the new transformer station, including cable type and cross-section selection.
- **Transformer station equipment sizing:** Short-circuit current calculations and selection of protection and associated equipment, including circuit breakers, fuses, disconnect switches, current transformers, metering, and earthing.
- **Execution plans:** Network layout and execution plans for both the LV and MV systems, created using AutoCAD.
- **Cost estimation:** Detailed quantitative and cost estimation of the works based on ONEE's approved price list.

## Tools Used

- AutoCAD — network layout and execution plans
- ONEE technical standards and reference tables — cable characteristics, equipment selection, and price references
